Barrier-Free Design Requirements

NBC 2023 AE Subsection 3.8.3 — verify each item during design development.

Universal Washroom — 3.8.3.13

Served by a barrier-free path of travel
Door: lever/loop hardware 900–1100 mm AFF; lockable inside, emergency release outside
One lavatory conforming to 3.8.3.16; one WC conforming to 3.8.3.14 (centreline 460–480 mm from side wall)
Clear lateral transfer space: ≥ 1500 mm from rear wall × ≥ 900 mm from WC seat edge
L-shaped grab bar on side wall: horizontal + vertical ≥ 760 mm; horizontal 750–850 mm AFF; vertical 150 mm in front of WC
Rear grab bar: ≥ 600 mm centred over WC (or 2 × 300 mm either side of flush valve)
Coat hook ≤ 1200 mm AFF; TP dispenser 600–800 mm AFF, closest edge 300 mm from front of WC
Shelf ≤ 1200 mm AFF (unless counter provided); wheelchair turning circle ≥ 1700 mm diameter
Power door operator required at entrance — NBC 3.8.2.7.(1)(c)

Accessible WC — 3.8.3.14

Seat height: 430–460 mm AFF
Flush: automatic or lever/loop at 500–900 mm AFF, ≤ 350 mm from transfer side
Seat lid or back support; securely attached tank top where tank-type

Accessible WC Stall — 3.8.3.12  (1 per 10 stalls or part thereof — 3.8.2.8.(5))

Wheelchair turning space ≥ 1500 mm diameter; clear floor in front: 1700 × 1700 mm
Door: lever hardware 900–1100 mm; clear opening ≥ 850 mm; outward swing; self-closing ≤ 50 mm ajar; D-pull inside ≥ 140 mm at 200–300 mm from hinge, 800–1000 mm AFF
WC centreline 460–480 mm from side wall; L-shaped grab bar on side wall + rear bar

Stall for Limited Mobility — 3.8.3.15  (1 per 10 stalls or part thereof — 3.8.2.8.(7))

Stall: ≥ 1500 mm deep × 890–940 mm wide
Door: lever hardware; clear opening ≥ 850 mm; outward swing unless no overlap; self-closing; pulls both sides 900–1100 mm AFF
WC conforming to 3.8.3.14, centred; horizontal grab bar each side 750–850 mm AFF, begins ≤ 300 mm from rear wall, extends ≥ 450 mm in front of seat

Accessible Urinal — 3.8.3.15(2)  (1 per 10 urinals or part thereof — 3.8.2.8.(6))

Wall-mounted; rim ≤ 430 mm AFF; no step in front
Clear approach: ≥ 800 mm wide × 1350 mm long, centred, unobstructed
Flush: automatic or lever 900–1100 mm AFF; vertical grab bar each side ≥ 600 mm, centreline 1000 mm AFF, ≤ 380 mm from urinal centreline

Accessible Lavatory & Mirror — 3.8.3.16

Faucets: automatic or lever/loop; no continuous-force hold; metered ≥ 10 s
Centreline ≥ 460 mm from side wall; rim ≤ 865 mm AFF; clear floor: ≥ 800 mm wide × 1350 mm long (≤ 430 mm under lav)
Under-sink clearance: 800 mm wide; 735 mm at front edge; 685 mm at 200 mm back; 230 mm over 280–430 mm range; pipes insulated
Soap dispenser: automatic or lever ≤ 1100 mm AFF within 500 mm of front; towel/dryer ≤ 1200 mm AFF
Mirror: bottom edge ≤ 1000 mm AFF or inclined for wheelchair use

Change Space — 3.8.2.8.(15) / 3.8.3.13.(2)  (Groups A / B-Div.2 / E with OL > 500)

One universal WR on barrier-free entrance storey shall include adult change table
Clear space: 810 × 1830 mm (non-overlapping); transfer space adjacent to long side: 900 × 1350 mm
