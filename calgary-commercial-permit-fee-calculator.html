Calgary commercial building permit fee calculator

Mirrors the City of Calgary commercial building permit fee calculator, then adds PDG's 9% admin handling fee on the City fees.

Step 1 — Base permit fee
$

Rounded up to the next $100. Rate: $10.14 per $1,000, plus $112.00 minimum processing fee.

Step 2 — Additional fees
m²

Building area as defined by the Alberta Building Code. $1.44/m², min $112.00, max $4,420.00. Leave blank if no partial permit is requested.

ha

Commercial/Industrial lot grading, required on all new buildings. Rounded up to the next whole hectare, $80.00 per hectare.

Safety Codes Council fee: 4% of the base permit fee (min $4.50, max $560.00), and 4% of the partial permit rate if one is requested (min $4.50, max $560.00).
